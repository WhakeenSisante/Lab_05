The Positionless Game

In today's league, positions like "Point Guard" or "Center" don't tell the whole story. Teams now look for specific archetypes to build their rosters. This project was created to showcase the visual difference between these roles using interactive elements. Understanding these roles helps fans and analysts see the game differently.

Archetype Definitions

3 & D Specialist

The "3 & D" player is perhaps the most sought-after role player in the modern era. These players specialize in two specific areas: hitting three-point shots at a high percentage and providing elite perimeter defense. They are the glue that holds championship rotations together.

Rim Runner

A Rim Runner is typically a mobile big man whose primary offensive job is to "run the floor" in transition and set hard screens. Once the screen is set, they immediately dive toward the basket to catch lobs. Defensively, they serve as the last line of defense, protecting the paint.

Pure Scorer

The Pure Scorer is a player with an advanced offensive package, capable of getting a bucket from anywhere on the court. Whether through isolation, pull-up jumpers, or navigating the lane, these players have the "green light" to take difficult shots.

Rebounder

A Rebounder is a high-motor player who excels at tracking the flight of the ball and securing possession after a missed shot. These players possess elite "box-out" techniques, strength, and timing to out-position opponents in the paint. Whether it's an Offensive Rebound to grant their team a second-chance opportunity or a Defensive Rebound to end the opponent's possession, they are the backbone of a team's physical presence.

Pure Point Guard

The Pure Point Guard is the "Quarterback" of the basketball court. Unlike scoring guards who look for their own shot first, the Pure PG prioritizes facilitation and game management. They possess elite "Court Vision," allowing them to see passing lanes before they even open, and a high "Basketball IQ" to manipulate the defense into mistakes. Their primary goal is to control the tempo of the game and ensure the ball reaches the right teammate at the perfect moment for an easy basket.

Point Forward

A Point Forward is a versatile player who has the size and strength of a forward but the ball-handling and passing skills of a point guard. By acting as the primary initiator, they create massive mismatches—they are too big for traditional guards to defend and too fast for traditional big men to keep up with. This archetype allows a team to run their offense through a player who can also crash the boards and guard multiple positions on the other end.
